Questions, answered

Before we begin

What does Eleene create?

Eleene is an AI creative studio. It creates surreal, photographic-grade product and campaign visuals, cinematic AI films and ads, AI-built websites and AI mobile apps — for luxury brands in fashion, beauty, bridal, hospitality and lifestyle that want to look as extraordinary online as they are in person.

Will my real product stay accurate?

Yes. Your product is built from your own reference photos, so its shape, materials, colour and label stay faithful. Every image is reviewed and retouched by hand before it reaches you.

Do I need to ship products or organise a shoot?

No. Clear reference photos of your product — plus brand guidelines if you have them — are enough to begin. No studio hire, travel, models or location fees.

What does a commission cost?

Every commission is quoted individually after a short conversation about scope, the number of visuals or length of film, and where they will be used. Send a message on Instagram and we will start there.

Can Eleene create my website or app too?

Yes — and it is one of the loveliest parts of the work. Your imagery, your website and your app can be created together as one coherent world, so everything your clients touch feels like your brand.

How do we start?

Send a direct message to @eleene.ai on Instagram with your brand, what you would like to create and your timing. Every message receives a personal reply.
