Questions, answered

Before we begin

What does Eleene create?

Eleene is my AI creative studio. I create AI product and campaign visuals, cinematic AI films and ads, AI-built websites and AI mobile apps — for brands in fashion, beauty, bridal, hospitality and lifestyle that want to look as premium online as they are in person.

Will my real product stay accurate?

Yes. Your product is built from your own reference photos, so its shape, materials, colour and label stay faithful. Every image is reviewed and retouched by hand before it reaches you.

Do I need to ship products or organise a shoot?

No. Clear reference photos of your product — plus brand guidelines if you have them — are enough to begin. No studio hire, travel, models or location fees.

What does a commission cost?

Every commission is quoted individually after a short conversation about scope, the number of visuals or length of film, and where they will be used. Send a message on Instagram and we will start there.

Can you build my website or app too?

Yes. Before becoming an AI creative director I was a mobile engineer with more than forty apps shipped, so the same studio can create your imagery, your website and your app — one coherent world.

How do we start?

Send a direct message to @eleene.ai on Instagram with your brand, what you would like to create and your timing. I reply personally.
