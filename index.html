Questions, answered

Before we begin

What can I commission?

Eleene creates product and campaign visuals, cinematic films, editorial imagery, websites and mobile apps. Projects can range from a single creative concept to a coordinated campaign or digital launch.

Will my real product stay accurate?

Product accuracy is a priority. I work from reference images, product details and brand guidelines, then review and refine the results to preserve recognizable shapes, materials, colours and branding. If exact reproduction is essential, we'll agree on the appropriate production method before starting.

Do I need a physical shoot?

Not always. Many projects can be developed using supplied references and approved digital assets. The right production approach depends on the required accuracy, creative direction and deliverables.

What does a project cost?

Every project is different. Pricing depends on scope, creative complexity, deliverables, formats and revisions. Once I understand your idea, I'll prepare a tailored proposal with a quote and timeline.

Do you create real, working websites and apps?

Yes. Digital projects include design and development, not just visual concepts. The precise technical scope, integrations, deployment and ongoing support are agreed before production.

Can we collaborate internationally?

Yes. Projects can be planned, reviewed and delivered remotely, with communication and milestones agreed at the beginning.

Can I use the final assets commercially?

Intended usage, licensing, delivery formats and any third-party restrictions are clarified in the project agreement. Commercial rights depend on the agreed deliverables and applicable asset and tool licences.

What happens after I get in touch?

Share a little about your brand and what you'd like to create. I'll review the brief and we can discuss the direction, scope and next steps.
